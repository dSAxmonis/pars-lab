How Does the Recommender Predict Ratings for a Movie with ZERO Historical Ratings?

The Item Cold-Start Challenge: Traditional collaborative filtering (user-user or matrix factorization) cannot recommend a brand-new item because its rating row/column is completely empty ($\sum_{u} R_{u, \text{new}} = 0$).

The Attribute-Based Content Solution: We project the new item into the shared attribute feature space using its metadata (Director: Christopher Nolan, Genres: Sci-Fi/Action/Thriller, Keywords: quantum, simulation, spacetime). We compute its cosine similarity $\text{Sim}(\text{new}, j)$ against all 20 existing catalog items, find its nearest neighbors, and project the user's expected rating:

Predicted Rating r̂(u, new) = ∑j ∈ Neighbors [ Sim(new, j) × r(u, j) ] / ∑j Sim(new, j)
