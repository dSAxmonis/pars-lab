SECTION 4: MATHEMATICAL FORMULATION & PENALTY MECHANISMS

L2 Regularization (Ridge Penalty):

Adds squared Euclidean magnitude of weights to the cost function:

J_reg(w, b) = J(w, b) + [ λ / (2m) ] × ∑ (w_j)²

Gradient Update: w_j := w_j (1 - αλ/m) - α [∂J/∂w_j]
Shrinks all weights proportionally towards zero (Weight Decay).

L1 Regularization (Lasso Penalty):

Adds absolute magnitude of coefficients to the cost function:

J_reg(w, b) = J(w, b) + [ λ / m ] × ∑ |w_j|

Gradient Update: w_j := w_j - α [∂J/∂w_j] - αλ/m × sgn(w_j)
Drives non-essential feature weights strictly to zero (Sparse Feature Selection).
